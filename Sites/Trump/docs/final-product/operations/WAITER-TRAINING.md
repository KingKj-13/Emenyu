# WAITER-TRAINING.md — Using the Trump Waiter App

**For:** waiters. You use the **Trump Waiter** app on an Android phone. No technical knowledge needed. ~10 minutes to learn.

---

## 1. Logging in
1. Open the **Trump Waiter** app.
2. Enter **your own** username + password → **Sign in**.
3. You stay logged in between shifts; the app remembers you. (If it ever asks again, just sign in.)
> Use your own account — never someone else's. It keeps your tables and tips/orders correct.

## 2. Starting your shift
- On **Home** or **My Shift**, tap **Start shift**. Now you're "on duty" and can take tables.
- You'll see your shift stats build up (orders, etc.) as you go.

## 3. Taking tables
- **My Tables** shows tables you own and other tables.
- To take a table, open it and tap **Take over** (with an optional reason). It's now yours.
- The screen always shows the truth from the server — what you see is real.

## 4. Orders
- Customers order from their phones (QR code) — those orders reach you automatically.
- If you place/confirm an order in the app, tap once. **Don't panic-tap** — even if you tap twice, it won't create a double order (the system prevents duplicates). If something didn't seem to go through, check before re-doing it.

## 5. Notifications
- The **bell** shows alerts: a table calling you, a table transferred to you, etc.
- Tap an alert to open the table. Tap to **acknowledge** when you've handled it.
- You'll also get a **phone notification** even if the app is in your pocket — open it to jump to the table.

## 6. Transferring a table
- Going on break or handing over? Open the table → **Transfer** → choose the waiter → confirm.
- They get notified and the table becomes theirs. (A manager can also move tables.)

## 7. Closing your shift
- At the end, tap **End shift**. Hand over or close out your tables first.
- Your shift stats are saved.

## 8. Offline behaviour (weak Wi-Fi)
- If you walk out of Wi-Fi range, the app keeps showing your last info and shows an **"offline / last synced"** banner.
- **While offline, action buttons are greyed out** ("reconnect to act") — this is on purpose, so two people can't grab the same table by accident.
- When you're back in range, it reconnects on its own and catches up. Just wait a moment.

## 9. Common mistakes (and the fix)
| Mistake | Fix |
|---|---|
| Logging in as someone else | Always use your own account |
| Double-tapping when it's slow | One tap is enough; duplicates are blocked, but wait for it to respond |
| Acting while offline | Buttons grey out on purpose — reconnect, then act |
| Forgetting to start/end shift | Start at the beginning, end at the end — it affects reports |
| Phone notifications off | Allow notifications when the app asks, or you'll miss table calls |
| Low battery mid-shift | Charge before service; the app uses normal app battery |

## 10. If the app misbehaves (Rule 1)
- If the app freezes or won't connect and customers are waiting: **serve them the normal way and tell your manager.** Don't let the app slow down service.
- Close and reopen the app; check your Wi-Fi. If still broken, the manager/operator handles it.
- **Lost your phone?** Tell your manager **immediately** so they can revoke it — no one can use your account after that.

**You're trained when you can:** log in, start a shift, take and transfer a table, handle an order + a notification, work through a brief offline moment, end your shift, and know to fall back if the app gets in the way.
