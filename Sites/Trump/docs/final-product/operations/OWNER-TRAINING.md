# OWNER-TRAINING.md — Running Your Restaurant on Trump

**For:** the restaurant owner. No technical knowledge needed. You sign in on a web browser (laptop/tablet) at **`https://emenyu.com/Trump`**. Your role is **Owner** — you can do everything.

---

## 1. Opening the restaurant
1. Make sure the internet/Wi-Fi is on and the QR codes are on the tables.
2. Open `https://emenyu.com/Trump` and **log in** with your owner username + password.
3. You land on the **Admin/Owner console**. Glance at the **Operations dashboard**: who's on shift, open tables, today's orders/revenue. If it loads, you're open for business.

## 2. Logging in
- Username + password → **Sign in**. If you forget your password, the operator can reset it ([PASSWORD-ROTATION.md](PASSWORD-ROTATION.md)).
- Keep your password private. Don't share the owner login — give staff their **own** accounts (below).

## 3. Managing staff
Console → **Manage staff**:
- **Add** a waiter / manager / kitchen account (each person gets their own login).
- **Suspend** someone instantly (e.g., they left) — they can no longer log in.
- **Reset** a password.
- Roles: **Owner** (you, everything) › **Manager** (run the floor) › **Waiter** (tables/orders) › **Kitchen** (sees orders).
> Give every staff member their own account — never share logins. It keeps reports + accountability accurate.

## 4. Editing the menu
Console → **Menu**:
- Add/edit items, prices, categories; hide an item that's sold out; add photos/videos.
- Changes appear to customers within a few seconds (the menu is cached briefly for speed).
- Recommendations ("chef recommends") can be set per item.

## 5. During service
- The **Operations dashboard** shows live: active waiters, open tables, orders, revenue.
- **Notifications bell** (top): waiter calls, table transfers, alerts.
- You don't need to do anything for orders to flow — waiters and the kitchen handle them. You watch and step in if needed.

## 6. Viewing reports
Console → **Reports/Analytics**:
- **Summary** (revenue, order count, average, busiest table), **Top items**, **Per-table**, **By hour**.
- Pick a date range. **At close, sanity-check the total against your till.**

## 7. Closing service
- Make sure waiters **end their shifts** (or a manager ends them).
- Pull the day's **reports**; confirm totals.
- That's it — data is saved automatically and backed up overnight.

## 8. Backups (peace of mind)
- Trump backs up your data **every night automatically**, with an off-site copy. You don't run anything.
- Your operator verifies backups monthly ([BACKUP-VERIFICATION.md](BACKUP-VERIFICATION.md)). Ask them for the monthly sign-off.

## 9. Common problems (and what to do)
| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| A staff member can't log in | Check they're using their own account; reset their password (Manage staff) |
| Menu won't load for a customer | Check the restaurant Wi-Fi; try again; if widespread, call your operator |
| Numbers look wrong | Note the table/time; tell your operator with the detail |
| The app/site is slow or down | **Fall back to your normal workflow (paper/till) and keep serving** — then call your operator |

## 10. Emergency procedures (Rule 1: the restaurant comes first)
- **If Trump ever gets in the way of serving customers, stop using it and go back to your normal workflow.** No order is lost — once it's back, it catches up.
- Keep your operator's number handy. For anything technical (server down, data wrong), that's their job ([SERVER-RECOVERY.md](SERVER-RECOVERY.md) / [INCIDENT-RESPONSE.md](INCIDENT-RESPONSE.md)).
- Lost a staff phone? Tell your operator to **revoke** it immediately (Manage devices) and reset that person's password.

**You're trained when you can:** open + log in, add/suspend a staff member, edit a menu item, watch the dashboard during service, pull and trust the end-of-day report, and know how to fall back if needed.
