# MANAGER-TRAINING.md — Running the Floor on Trump

**For:** managers. You run service: assign/transfer tables, watch staff, handle problems, check reports. You use the **web console** (`https://emenyu.com/Trump`) and can also use the waiter app. Your role is **Manager** (everything except managing owners).

---

## 1. Logging in
- Your own manager username + password at `https://emenyu.com/Trump` → the Admin console + Operations dashboard.

## 2. Table ownership
- See **every table** and who owns it on the Operations / Tables view.
- **Assign** an unowned table to a waiter; **reassign** a table from one waiter to another (a reason is required — it's recorded).
- This is your main lever during a rush: balance tables across waiters.

## 3. Transfers & take-overs
- Waiters transfer their own tables; **you** can move any table (reassign) — e.g., a waiter went home or is overwhelmed.
- Every change is **logged** (who, from→to, reason) so nothing is ambiguous later.

## 4. Monitoring staff
- **Operations dashboard:** active waiters, who's on shift, open tables, orders, revenue, per-waiter performance.
- If a waiter forgot to **start/end** a shift, you can end a shift for them.
- Watch for a table sitting unowned or a waiter overloaded → reassign.

## 5. Notifications
- The **bell** aggregates floor alerts (waiter calls, transfers, special-occasion/AI alerts).
- You can also **send** a notification to a specific waiter or role (e.g., "VIP at table 5") from the console.
- Acknowledge/clear handled alerts.

## 6. Reports
- Same analytics as the owner: **Summary, Top items, Per-table, By hour**, date-range filtered.
- Use them mid-service (how's the pace?) and at close (sanity-check totals vs. till).

## 7. Problem handling (during service — Rule 1)
| Situation | Do |
|---|---|
| A waiter's app won't connect | Have them serve normally; you cover their tables from the console; tell the operator |
| Orders look stuck | Check the kitchen/orders view; if widespread, **fall back to normal workflow** and call the operator |
| A table is double-owned or unowned | Reassign it to the right waiter (with a reason) |
| Suspected duplicate/lost order | Note table + time; check orders/history; capture details for the operator (it shouldn't happen — duplicates are blocked) |
| A staff phone is lost | **Revoke the device** (Manage devices) + ask the operator to reset that password |

## 8. Opening / closing duties
- **Open:** confirm staff are logged in and on shift; tables/QR ready; dashboard healthy.
- **Close:** ensure all shifts ended and tables closed out; pull reports; confirm totals; flag anything odd to the owner/operator.

## 9. What you can and can't do
- ✅ Assign/reassign tables, end shifts, send notifications, view all reports, manage waiter/kitchen accounts.
- ❌ You cannot manage **owner** accounts. Deep technical issues (server down, data recovery) are the **operator's** job → [INCIDENT-RESPONSE.md](INCIDENT-RESPONSE.md).

**You're trained when you can:** monitor the floor on the dashboard, assign/reassign a table with a reason, end a shift for a waiter, send and clear a notification, pull reports, and handle a service hiccup by falling back + escalating.
